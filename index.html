Hello World! I am Jacky Li, an undergraduate student at The Hong Kong University of Science and Technology. I am affiliated with the Cheng Kar-Shun Robotics Institute and the Multi-Agent Systems Laboratory, where I am fortunate to work closely with Prof. Ling Shi.

Previously, I was a Visiting Student Researcher at UC Berkeley’s MSC Lab, advised by Prof. Masayoshi Tomizuka. I continue this collaboration remotely, with much of my current effort devoted to our ongoing work.

My research focuses on reinforcement learning, learning-based control, multi-agent systems, and robotics. I am particularly interested in developing learning-based methods for unmanned robotic systems operating in complex real-world environments.

Alongside my research, I am part of Universpirit and co-founded NaviHK.

I was born in Hong Kong 🇭🇰. I am fluent in English, Cantonese, and Mandarin, and I can also speak and understand Japanese.

I enjoy mathematics. I am not gifted at it; I simply love its logic—the journey from axioms to proofs—and the beauty and elegance of mathematical tools. As a small piece of mathematical trivia, my Erdős number is 4 (see the Erdős Number Project).

My mother is a teacher. Perhaps I inherited a knack for it: I have felt at ease presenting ideas since childhood, and I am quite good at explaining concepts and teaching others.

香港で生まれました 🇭🇰。英語、広東語、普通話を流暢に話し、日本語も話したり聞き取ったりできます。

私には数学の才能があるわけではありません。それでも数学が好きなのは、公理から証明へと至る論理、そして数学的な道具が持つ美しさとエレガンスに惹かれているからです。ちなみに、私のエルデシュ数は4です（Erdős Number Project を参照）。

母は教師です。その影響もあってか、幼い頃から人前で考えを伝えることが得意で、概念を分かりやすく説明したり人に教えたりすることにも自信があります。
